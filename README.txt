---- Quake 4 Stealth Mod ----

A mod that applies stealth mechanics to the base game of Quake 4. Beside the SDK and codebase, a PAK001.pk4 file with custom dependencies (def files, guis, etc.) is available as well. Ensure that this pak file is in the same file as the mod folder. As for the mod itself, there are numerous new features to be aware of:

1. Superpowers: The player has access to superpowers to aid them in their stealth/evasion. Superspeed (T) allows the player to move at lightning speed - great for escape or bolting pass enemies unseen. Invisibility (Y) prevents enemies from seeing the player, allowing them to walk by unnoticed. Teleportation (U) allows the player to teleport to the nearest enemy for an easy takedown. Invincibility (I) makes the player impervious for damage - handy for when the player is caught unexpected. Drain (O) drains the health of the nearest enemy while healing you slightly. All of these powers have cooldowns show by color-coded timers on the screen. Moreover, the color for each respective superpower will blink at the beginning and end of its use.

2. Weapons: The player has access to a ton of new weapons based off the metal gear solid series. The SOCOM is a pistol that cannot be charged but can get a suppressor to make it silent - it will not trigger enemies! The FAMAS is a burst assault rifle (three shots) that can also get a suppressor. The PSG-1 is a sniper rifle that can be shot without zoom (with terrible accuracy) and with zoom, dealing large damage. Unlike the SOCOM and the FAMAS, however, this weapon is NOT silent and WIll alert nearby enemies. The Nikita is a missile launcher that allows the user to guide the missile after launch. The C4 launcher shoots projectiles that can be detonated after pressing TAB, but will only detonate that last projectile that was launched. The Stim Grenade heals the player after launch, but will only heal the player to 120 health. The Impulse Grenade sends the player backward, helping them escape when other superpowers are exhausted. The Stun Grenade stuns enemies and place for a brief period, preventing them from seeing you. The Chaff Grenade does NOT stun enemies but disables their guns. They will still see you, and will certainly shoot you once their guns are back online. The Single Action Army is a revolver that shoots slower, cannot be suppressed, but does high damage - useful when only a single enemy is left. Finally, the M61 Vulcan is a hand-held turret, or more accurately, a death machine. It will cut through enemies like Swiss cheese and gib them on impact. 

3. Items: The player has access to five items that help them sneak. They can get two suppressors - one for the SOCOM and one for the FAMAS. The AP Sensor can detect with enemies are nearby, useful for determining if an enemy is around the corner or if teleportation is possible and safe. The Cardboard Box allows the player to hide inside, preventing enemies from seeing you. Diazepam is a drug that makes the player shoot more accurately - a deadly combination with the PSG-1.

With these tools at your disposable, sneaking by enemies should be a piece of cake!
